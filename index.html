HOW TO PLAY

Touch and drag anywhere to move. Tap the SPLIT bar at the bottom to split.

You can eat anything you're at least 20% bigger than. A green glow means it's small enough that even your split-off hunter could eat it. Run from anything with a red glow; it can eat you.

Your score is your final mass: everything you're carrying when your last piece gets eaten. Records are also kept for peak mass and longest game.

At 100 mass, tap SPLIT: 60% stays put, 40% goes hunting faster. Touch your stationary half to merge again.
